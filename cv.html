Research Vision

My long-term research vision is to develop intelligent power electronic systems that combine advanced converter technologies with Artificial Intelligence to improve the efficiency, reliability, and resilience of future energy infrastructures. My goal is to contribute to next-generation wireless power transfer, autonomous electric transportation, and smart energy systems through interdisciplinary research that bridges theoretical innovation with practical engineering implementation.
