Research

My research interests focus on advanced power conversion, wireless power transfer, resonant power electronics, electric vehicle charging, and energy systems.

Research Overview

My research combines analytical modeling, simulation, power electronic design, optimization, and experimental investigation of advanced electrical energy conversion systems. A particular focus is placed on high-efficiency power transfer, resonant conversion, and technologies supporting electrified transportation.

Research Areas

Wireless Power Transfer

Research on resonant inductive wireless power transfer, magnetic coupling, compensation networks, system modeling, efficiency optimization, and robust power transfer under practical operating conditions.

Power Electronics

Research involving power electronic converters, resonant power conversion, converter analysis, control strategies, and high-efficiency electrical energy conversion.

Electric Vehicle Charging

Investigation of charging technologies for electric vehicles, including wireless charging architectures, power conversion stages, magnetic coupling, and system-level efficiency.

Resonant Power Conversion

Analysis and design of resonant converters and resonant compensation networks for efficient power conversion and wireless energy transfer.

Energy Systems

Advanced energy conversion and management approaches for efficient and sustainable electrical energy systems.

Modeling, Simulation & Optimization

Analytical modeling, numerical simulation, parameter optimization, performance evaluation, and comparison of advanced electrical and power electronic systems.

Research Keywords

Wireless Power Transfer Resonant Inductive Coupling Power Electronics Resonant Converters EV Charging Compensation Networks Magnetic Coupling Efficiency Optimization Energy Conversion Energy Management

Research Approach

  • Analytical modeling and mathematical analysis of power electronic and wireless power transfer systems.
  • Electromagnetic and circuit-level simulation for system evaluation and optimization.
  • Design and optimization of resonant power conversion and compensation networks.
  • Performance evaluation based on efficiency, power transfer capability, system robustness, and operating conditions.
  • Experimental investigation and validation of electrical energy conversion systems.

Future Research Directions

Potential directions for future research and collaboration include the development of efficient and robust wireless charging technologies and advanced power electronic systems for electrified transportation and sustainable energy systems.

EV Wireless Charging

High-efficiency wireless charging architectures and robust operation under practical conditions.

Advanced WPT Systems

Resonant architectures, compensation methods, coupling enhancement, and system optimization.

High-Efficiency Power Conversion

Advanced converter topologies and efficient energy conversion for modern electrical systems.

Research Collaboration

I am interested in research collaborations in wireless power transfer, power electronics, electric vehicle charging, resonant power conversion, and related electrical energy systems.
