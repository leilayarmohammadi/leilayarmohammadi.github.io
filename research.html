Research Profile

Research

My research focuses on advanced power electronics, wireless power transfer, resonant power conversion, electric vehicle charging, and intelligent energy systems.

Research Perspective

From Power Conversion to Wireless Energy Systems

My research in power electronics is centered on the analysis, design, optimization, and experimental validation of advanced power conversion systems.

A major focus of my doctoral research was the design and experimental implementation of a high-order resonant converter with load-independent output current for wireless power transfer systems.

My broader research interests extend to wireless charging technologies, resonant converters, electric vehicle charging, intelligent energy management, and the integration of advanced power electronic technologies with smart energy infrastructures.
