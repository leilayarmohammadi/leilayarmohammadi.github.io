About

Researcher & Electrical Engineer

I am a power electronics researcher, university lecturer, and senior electrical engineer with expertise in wireless power transfer, resonant power converters, intelligent energy systems, and smart infrastructure.

My research combines analytical modeling, optimization, simulation, and experimental validation to develop efficient and reliable power electronic systems.

I am particularly interested in research that connects advanced power conversion technologies with electric transportation and intelligent energy infrastructures.

Wireless Power Transfer & Resonant Power Conversion

High-Order Resonant Converter for Wireless Power Transfer

My doctoral research focused on the design and experimental implementation of a high-order resonant converter with load-independent output current for wireless power transfer systems.

  • Resonant converter topology and compensation
  • Load-independent output characteristics
  • Analytical modeling and parameter optimization
  • Simulation and experimental validation
