About Me

I am an Electrical Engineering faculty member and researcher with research interests spanning power electronics, wireless power transfer, resonant converters, electric vehicle charging, and energy systems.

My research combines analytical modeling, simulation, power electronic design, optimization, and experimental investigation of advanced energy conversion systems.

Research Interests

Wireless Power Transfer

Resonant inductive power transfer, compensation networks, coupling analysis, efficiency optimization, and system performance.

Power Electronics

Power electronic converters, resonant conversion, power factor correction, and high-efficiency power conversion.

EV Wireless Charging

Wireless charging technologies for electric vehicles, magnetic coupling, charging systems, and system optimization.

Energy Systems

Energy conversion, energy management strategies, and advanced systems for sustainable electrification.
