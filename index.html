Research Focus

My research focuses on the development and optimization of efficient power conversion and wireless charging technologies.

Particular interests include resonant wireless power transfer, compensation networks, EV wireless charging, power electronic converters, and experimental validation.

About Me

I am an Electrical Engineering researcher and faculty member with research experience in power electronics, wireless power transfer, resonant converters, electric vehicle charging, and energy management.

My research combines analytical modeling, simulation, optimization, power electronic design, and experimental investigation of advanced energy conversion systems.

Research Interests

Wireless Power Transfer

Resonant inductive power transfer, compensation networks, load-independent operation, efficiency optimization, and system analysis.

Power Electronics

Power electronic converters, resonant conversion, power factor correction, and high-efficiency power conversion.

EV Wireless Charging

Wireless charging technologies for electric vehicles, magnetic structures, coupling analysis, and charging systems.

Energy Management

Energy conversion, energy management strategies, and intelligent systems for sustainable electrification.
